How Security Analysts Use These Rules

While the Random Forest produces higher accuracy (92.84%), it acts as an ensemble black box. The Decision Tree rules provide a verifiable audit trail:

  • Root Split: READ_PHONE_STATE is the single most influential differentiator.
  • Second Level: Combining phone state with c2dm.permission.RECEIVE separates cloud-push apps from background collectors.
  • Third Level: Presence of INSTALL_SHORTCUT, BILLING, or SEND_SMS triggers definitive threat branching.
